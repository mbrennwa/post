Post

Post is free and open source. It uses your online accounts from GNOME Online Accounts and works with IMAP, Gmail, Microsoft 365, and local machine mail. It does one thing, and does it well: email — no calendars, no to-dos, no newsfeeds, no contacts manager, no bloat.

Post is in active development. Do not assume it is bulletproof for productive environments.
